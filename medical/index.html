Straight Answers

Questions Medical Offices Actually Ask

Will you sign a BAA?

Yes — for every medical plan, before go-live. That's part of what the $600/mo HIPAA compliance rider pays for. If a vendor won't sign one, they shouldn't be near your phones.

What does the AI actually collect from patients?

Only what it takes to book and route the appointment. It is not trying to take a history. Less sensitive information collected means less sitting around to be exposed later.

Where do the call transcripts live?

Encrypted storage, with access restricted to people who need it and a record of who opened what. That record is the part a general answering service usually cannot give you.

Is there any way to reduce the setup fee?

No. Setup is priced at cost and is never discounted, promoted, or negotiated. That is a deliberate policy, not a negotiating posture.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Month-to-month — cancel anytime. Your first monthly payment isn't due until 30 days after your AI goes live.

One honest note. No vendor can make your practice compliant on its own — compliance depends on how your office operates as a whole. What we do is make sure the AI answering your phones is built, papered, and configured so it strengthens your posture instead of quietly undermining it. Review the specifics with your own counsel or compliance officer before you go live.
