The Compliance Gap

A Regular AI Receptionist
Is a Liability in Your Office.

Standard AI answering services were never designed to touch protected health information or privileged client communications. If yours does, the exposure lands on your practice — not the vendor. That's why medical and law offices get their own build, their own safeguards, and their own pricing.

🏥

Medical Offices

Almost every call to your office starts with a patient explaining a symptom — which makes almost every call protected health information. Most AI answering services keep those recordings and transcripts on infrastructure you can't see, with no Business Associate Agreement and no record of who read what. If it ever comes up in an audit, it lands on your practice, not the vendor.

We sign a BAA before your line goes live. Transcripts are encrypted, access is restricted and logged, and the receptionist asks only what it takes to book and route the appointment — so there's less sensitive information sitting around to begin with.

⚖️

Law Offices

A generic answering bot can create real problems for your firm before you ever pick up the phone — an implied attorney-client relationship, an answer that sounds like legal advice, or an intake on a matter you're conflicted out of. Any one of those is a bar complaint waiting to happen.

Ours screens for conflicts early in the call, never offers an opinion on a caller's situation, and tells every caller plainly that nothing is attorney-client until your firm accepts the matter. Intake records stay confidential and are ready to produce if anyone ever asks.

Straight Answers

Questions Practices Actually Ask

Will you sign a BAA?

Yes — for every medical plan, before go-live. The signed BAA and HIPAA-aware safeguards are included in the service.

Does the AI give legal advice?

No. It takes down the caller's information, screens for conflicts, tells them plainly that no attorney-client relationship exists until your firm accepts the matter, and hands them to you. If a caller pushes for an opinion on their case, it says that's a question for the attorney and gets them to you instead.

Is the email chatbot included?

Yes. Voice reception, website chat, and an email chatbot are included in the service. It can answer approved administrative questions, gather preliminary intake details, guide prospects toward booking, and escalate sensitive messages to your team.

Is there any way to reduce the setup fee?

No. Setup is priced at cost on both plans and is never discounted, promoted, or negotiated. That's a deliberate policy, not a negotiating posture.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Both plans are month-to-month — cancel anytime. Your first monthly payment isn't due until 30 days after your AI goes live.

Who do I reach if something breaks?

A real person in the US. Support is human, not a ticket queue, and additional hands-on remote work is billed at $95/hr if you ever want it.

One honest note. No vendor can make your practice compliant on its own — compliance depends on how your office operates as a whole. What we do is make sure the AI answering your phones is built, papered, and configured so it strengthens your posture instead of quietly undermining it. Review the specifics with your own counsel or compliance officer before you go live.
