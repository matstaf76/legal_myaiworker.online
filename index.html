Straight Answers

What Modern Law Firms Need to Know

Is this meant to replace lawyers?

No. Legal.MyAIworker.online automates client engagement and routine practice workflows so attorneys can work faster while retaining legal judgment, strategy, and final review.

Does the AI give legal advice?

No. It takes down the caller's information, screens for conflicts, tells them plainly that no attorney-client relationship exists until your firm accepts the matter, and hands them to you. If a caller pushes for an opinion on their case, it says that's a question for the attorney and gets them to you instead.

Is the email chatbot included?

Yes. Voice reception, website chat, and an email chatbot are included in the service. It can answer approved administrative questions, gather preliminary intake details, guide prospects toward booking, and escalate sensitive messages to your team.

What does Legal AI implementation include?

Legal AI implementation is a one-time fee of $3,500. Monthly service is $1,250 for the first user plus $357 for each additional user. Employee count does not automatically equal user count; only people who need their own AI-enabled access or workflow count as users.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Service is month-to-month — cancel anytime. Your first recurring payment isn't due until 30 days after your AI goes live.

Who do I reach if something breaks?

A real person in the US. Support is human, not a ticket queue.

One honest note. No vendor can make your practice compliant on its own — compliance depends on how your office operates as a whole. What we do is make sure the AI answering your phones is built, papered, and configured so it strengthens your posture instead of quietly undermining it. Review the specifics with your own counsel or compliance officer before you go live.

Everything you need to know

Is Fake Law Firm a real law firm?

No. This is a fictional website built to demonstrate a law firm experience with an AI assistant. No attorneys, legal services, case results, or real appointments are offered here.

Can I get legal advice from Riley?

No. Riley is an AI demonstration guide, not an attorney. Interacting with Riley or this website does not create an attorney-client relationship. For a real matter, contact a qualified attorney directly.

What happens when I try the contact form?

The form opens a demonstration notice and clears the sample entries. It does not collect, save, or transmit your information, and it does not book a consultation.

Is my Riley conversation private?

Riley connects to a third-party voice AI service, which processes audio and messages. We cannot promise confidentiality or zero retention by that provider. Do not share personal, confidential, privileged, or sensitive details.

Can I keep browsing while I talk?

Yes. Section links scroll within this page. Minimizing Riley keeps the current call and transcript available. Use End call to stop the microphone session. Reloading or leaving the page ends the page session.
