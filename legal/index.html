Straight Answers

Questions Law Offices Actually Ask

Does the AI give legal advice?

No. It takes down the caller's information, screens for conflicts, tells them plainly that no attorney-client relationship exists until your firm accepts the matter, and hands them to you. If a caller pushes for an opinion on their case, it says that's a question for the attorney and gets them to you instead.

How does conflict screening work?

It asks the screening questions your firm specifies before it takes any detail about the matter, so a conflicted caller never gets far enough to create a problem.

Why is the legal rider more expensive than the medical one?

Legal intake carries heavier per-matter compliance obligations — conflict screening, privilege handling, and record retention — and the tooling behind it costs more to run. We show you the base and the rider separately so you can see exactly where the difference sits.

Is there any way to reduce the setup fee?

No. Setup is priced at cost and is never discounted, promoted, or negotiated. That is a deliberate policy, not a negotiating posture.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Month-to-month — cancel anytime. Your first monthly payment isn't due until 30 days after your AI goes live.

One honest note. No vendor can make your practice compliant on its own — compliance depends on how your office operates as a whole. What we do is make sure the AI answering your phones is built, papered, and configured so it strengthens your posture instead of quietly undermining it. Review the specifics with your own counsel or compliance officer before you go live.
